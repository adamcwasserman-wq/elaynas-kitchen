Put Tulip Kitchen on your home screen

Then it opens like an app, and works with no internet

  1. Open this page in Safari (the blue compass app).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add. Look for the tulip on your home screen!
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Install. Look for the tulip on your home screen!

Your game saves on your phone only. To keep it safe, make a backup now and then: Settings → Backup in the game.
